Scientific Poster

Featured research

Magnetic Ordering in Chiral Nanotubes with Competing Interactions

Itxaso Muñoz-Aldalur, Òscar Iglesias Clotas — Dept. Física de la Matèria Condensada, Universitat de Barcelona

This poster presents Monte Carlo simulations (classical 3D Heisenberg model) of single-wall magnetic nanotubes with competing exchange and dipolar interactions. By folding planar spin lattices at different chiral angles, we reveal a rich variety of stable magnetic states — uniform, vortex-like and helical configurations — depending on tube geometry, chirality and the exchange-to-dipolar ratio γ = g/J. Adding radial anisotropy further stabilizes radial and mixed magnetization textures, offering design insight for functional magnetic nanostructures. Presented at ICFPM 2025 (Bariloche) and as an oral talk at INTERMAG 2026 (Manchester).
